A meeting is now named by the occurrence it actually was, everywhere it is cited as a source: the To Do list, the meeting digest grid and detail pane, and a story's source-meeting label. Renaming a recurring Teams series no longer retitles every past occurrence that was recorded under the old name.

Meetings linked before this release are unaffected in how their action items were matched to work items; only the name shown alongside them changes.
