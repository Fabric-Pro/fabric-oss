---
title: Channels & Meetings as Context
description: Monitor Slack and Teams channels for backlog ideas, and sync Teams meeting transcripts into your project as context.
---

Bring the conversations your team is already having into a project. Fabric can **watch Slack and Teams channels** (and Teams group chats) and turn the discussion into proposed backlog items, and it can **sync Teams meeting transcripts** into your project as searchable context for AI.

You set all of this up per project under **Settings → Knowledge**.

<Callout type="info">
**How this differs from other integrations.** Runtime actions let an agent *call* Slack/Teams on demand ("post to Slack"). [PM Sync](/docs/features/pm-sync) keeps a backlog aligned with a PM tool. This is a third thing: **pulling conversation and meeting content in** as project context and backlog ideas. All three share the same connected integration.
</Callout>

## Channel & chat monitoring

Link a channel (or a Teams group chat) to a project, and Fabric analyzes new messages and **drafts proposed backlog changes** for you to review — nothing is added to your backlog automatically.

| Source | Connect | How it picks up messages |
|--------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Slack channel** | Slack integration | Event-driven — reacts to new messages in near real time |
| **Teams channel** | Microsoft Teams integration | Scheduled scans on an interval you choose |
| **Teams group chat** | Microsoft Teams integration | Scheduled scans on an interval you choose |

### Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open project knowledge settings

    In your project, go to **Settings → Knowledge** and find the **channel monitor** for Slack or Teams (Teams also has a separate **chat monitor** for group chats).
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Link channels

    Use the link button to open the channel (or chat) picker and choose which ones to monitor. The provider must already be connected under **Settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Turn on monitoring

    Enable the monitor. For Teams you also pick a **scan interval** and a quiet window; Slack reacts to messages as they arrive. You can also trigger a one-off scan to pull recent history immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Review proposals

    Fabric drafts **backlog change proposals** from the conversation. Review and **approve** the ones you want — only then do they become features in your backlog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Monitoring proposes; it never writes silently. Every suggestion goes through your review before it touches the backlog. Unlink a channel at any time to stop monitoring it.
</Callout>

## Teams meeting transcripts

Link recurring **Teams meetings** to a project and Fabric syncs their **transcripts** as project context, so AI features and agents can draw on what was actually said in the meeting.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Link meetings

    In **Settings → Knowledge**, open the meeting picker and link the recurring Teams meetings you want. Transcripts from roughly the **last 30 days** are pulled on the first sync.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Keep them in sync

    Turn on auto-sync to pull new transcripts on a schedule, or run a sync on demand. A **Last sync** timestamp shows when transcripts were last pulled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Synced transcripts become part of your project's knowledge, so they're available to [RAG retrieval](/docs/core-concepts/rag) the same way uploaded documents are.

<Callout type="info">
Meeting transcripts require that transcription is enabled and available for the meeting in Microsoft Teams. Channel/chat monitoring and meeting-transcript sync are configured separately, both under **Settings → Knowledge**.
</Callout>

## What's supported

| Capability | Slack | Microsoft Teams |
|-----------|:-----:|:---------------:|
| Channel monitoring → backlog proposals | ✅ | ✅ |
| Group-chat monitoring → backlog proposals | — | ✅ |
| Meeting transcripts → project context | — | ✅ |
