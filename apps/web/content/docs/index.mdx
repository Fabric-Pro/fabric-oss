## Ask your first question

Open **Genie** in the sidebar and type the question. Genie is the conversation for your organization. It answers from the project you have open, the context your team has added, and the tools connected to the organization.

You are always in an organization. There is no separate personal workspace to pick first.

## Open the work

Choose **Projects**. Open a project to read the spec, the coding instructions, and the tests, or to start a conversation that stays with that project.

## Use an agent your team already built

Choose **AI Agents**. Open an agent to read its instructions and the knowledge it can use.
