Connect Google Drive to Fabric AI so agents and workflows can list, search, and read your Drive documents — as live runtime actions, on demand.

Google Drive is a **Knowledge** provider: it offers read/retrieval actions, not write actions.

## Integration Methods

| Method | Use Case | Auth |
|--------|----------|------|
| **MCP Server** | List, search, and read Drive files in chat & agents | OAuth |
| **Workflow Plugin** | Access Drive files in workflow nodes | OAuth |

## Setting Up Google Drive

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Google Drive under Integrations

    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and open the **Google Drive** card.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Set Up Actions

    Click **Set Up Actions** and authorize Fabric AI on Google's consent screen. Sign in with the Google account whose Drive you want Fabric to access.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Confirm

    The provider shows **Connected** with its available runtime actions. Agents can now call them live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Using a Google Workspace account? Your admin may need to approve the Fabric OAuth app before you can connect.
</Callout>

## Available Actions

| Action | Type | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **List Drive Files** | Knowledge | List files and folders from Google Drive |
| **Search Drive** | Knowledge | Search for files in Google Drive |
| **Get Document Content** | Knowledge | Retrieve content from a Google Drive document |

### Using in AI Chat

```
"What does our company handbook say about remote work policies?"

"Summarize the Q4 financial report from Drive"

"Find the design specifications for the mobile app"
```

The agent calls Drive's knowledge actions live to find and read the relevant documents — there's no background sync to wait on.

## Multiple Accounts

Connect multiple Google accounts:

- Personal Google account for your own documents
- Organization shared drives for team content
- Each connection is identified by its account / workspace

## Troubleshooting

### OAuth consent errors
- Ensure you're signing in with the correct Google account
- If using a Google Workspace account, your admin may need to approve the OAuth app

### An action returns no results
- Confirm the connection shows **Connected** under **Settings → Integrations**
- Make sure the signed-in account has access to the files you expect
