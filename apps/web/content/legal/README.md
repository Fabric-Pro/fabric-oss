# Legal content

`privacy-policy.mdx` and `terms.mdx` are TechFabric LLC's legal text for the **hosted Fabric service at fabric.pro**. They live here because they are part of the application that serves fabric.pro — they are not a template.

**If you self-host Fabric, replace both files before you expose the deployment to anyone.** They render at `/legal/privacy-policy` and `/legal/terms`. Served unmodified, they tell your users that TechFabric LLC is the controller of their data and that privacy requests go to a mailbox that cannot action them — neither of which is true of your deployment. Each file opens with a scope notice saying so, but a notice is not a substitute for replacing the text.

Nothing here is legal advice, and none of it is offered as a template for anyone else's service. Have your own counsel produce your policy and terms.
