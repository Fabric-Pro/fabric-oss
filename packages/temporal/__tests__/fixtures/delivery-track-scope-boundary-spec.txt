## Feature Narrative

### Feature Story

As a task assignee,  
I want an email reminder when my task is due tomorrow,  
so that I can prepare to complete it by its due date.

### Overview

Add email reminders for tasks due on the next calendar day. Reminders target task assignees. Recipient-role eligibility, timezone, scheduling, user preferences, and delivery behavior require Product decisions before implementation can be completed.

### Benefit Hypothesis

If assignees receive reminders before their tasks are due,  
then fewer deadlines will be overlooked,  
because upcoming work is surfaced outside the task board.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Evaluate task due dates for next-day email reminders.
- Deliver reminders to eligible task assignees.
- Exclude undated tasks and tasks outside the next-day date boundary.
- Define recipient eligibility, reminder timing, email presentation, and preference behavior.
- Define handling of changed deadlines and repeated evaluations.

### Out of Scope

- Changes to the weekly progress summary or CSV export.
- Reminder channels other than email.
- Reminders for tasks due today, overdue tasks, or tasks due later than tomorrow.

## Key Decisions

- Task assignees are the intended recipients, as specified by the feature title and user description. Role-based restrictions remain unresolved.
- Tasks without a due date must not be presented as due tomorrow, consistent with TC-010.
- Tasks due today are excluded from next-day reminders, consistent with TC-007.
- Retrieved test cases explicitly defer unresolved delivery policies to Product; their fixtures are not approved configuration values.

## Use Cases

### UC1: Receive an upcoming-deadline reminder

- Primary actor: An eligible task assignee.
- Preconditions: The task has a due date; Product-approved timing, timezone, eligibility, and recipient rules are implemented.
- Main flow:
  1. The reminder process evaluates tasks at the approved execution time.
  2. It selects eligible tasks due on the next calendar day in the approved timezone.
  3. It sends emails to their eligible assignees using the approved grouping and content rules.
- Alternate flows / edge cases:
  - Undated tasks and tasks outside the next-day boundary are excluded.
  - Multiple qualifying tasks follow the approved grouping policy.
  - Tasks changed after an evaluation follow the approved late-change and repeat-notification rules.
- Expected outcome: The assignee receives reminders identifying the qualifying tasks.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

- FR1: Evaluate tasks for email reminders using the Product-approved schedule and timezone rule.
- FR2: Select tasks whose due date is the next calendar day relative to the evaluation date, subject to approved eligibility rules. Do not interpret “tomorrow” as an unspecified rolling 24-hour window.
- FR3: Exclude tasks with no due date, tasks due today, and tasks due outside tomorrow.
- FR4: Send reminders to eligible task assignees according to the approved role and preference policies.
- FR5: Represent every selected task in the delivered reminder content. Grouping and message counts require Product approval.
- FR6: Ensure reminder content distinguishes separate tasks with identical titles. The identifying fields or link mechanism require Product approval.
- FR7: Apply approved rules for due-date or assignee changes after evaluation and for previously reminded tasks that become eligible again.

### Permissions / Roles

- Assignee targeting is required; whether all assignee roles qualify remains unresolved.
- Per-user opt-out behavior remains unresolved.

### Data & Validation Rules

- Determine tomorrow using the approved timezone and calendar-date boundaries.
- Do not infer a due date for undated tasks.
- For timestamp-backed deadlines, apply the next-day interval from local midnight inclusive to the following local midnight exclusive.

## PM/BA Blocking Gaps

- Recipient and preference policy is incomplete.
  - Impact: Recipient selection and suppression cannot be finalized.
  - Decision needed / owner: Product must specify role eligibility, opt-out behavior, and handling of unassigned tasks or assignees without deliverable email addresses.
- Evaluation and eligibility policy is incomplete.
  - Impact: Selection boundaries, scheduling, and lifecycle-state tests cannot be finalized.
  - Decision needed / owner: Product must approve timezone, execution timing, and eligible task states.
- Email presentation is unapproved.
  - Impact: Message counts, content, task identification, and empty-result behavior cannot be finalized.
  - Decision needed / owner: Product must approve grouping, template content, and whether an empty email is sent.
- Late-change and repeat-notification policy is incomplete.
  - Impact: Rescheduling and repeated evaluation outcomes cannot be finalized; TC-011 remains blocked.
  - Decision needed / owner: Product must approve these behaviors.
